# Live QA, 8 November 2026

> The screenshots this report was first written with are no longer in the repository: they showed other people's videos and thumbnails.

The app at bfbaf94 (Phase 4 complete) against real YouTube, SponsorBlock and DeArrow, signed out, on iPhone 17 and iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulators with iOS 26.2. The simulator cannot be touched, so three testers drove the app through its DEBUG hooks (`-openURL`, `-uiScript`, `-playerScript`, `-watchPage`, `-axDump` and others) and judged screenshots. They changed no code. The defects they found were fixed and merged on main (882f19c and abbabff), then re-checked live on abbabff.

## Results

| Area | Pass | Fail | Skipped | Defects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Watching and playback | 21 | 0 | 6 | 0 |
| Library, downloads, settings, links | 26 | 2 | 6 | 3 |
| Layouts and accessibility (iPad, largest text, dark mode, labels) | 17 | 3 | 4 | 6 |

Confirmed working, among others: the welcome card and topic chips; search results without ads or Shorts, more pages, the filters sheet, pasted and Shorts links; the watch page with description links, chapters, comments and replies; playback, pause, seeking, quality, speed and captions; the mini player; resume; SponsorBlock auto-skip with Undo and coloured segments; De-clickbait titles; Play all with "Playing from" and Next; the error screen (Retry only where it helps, Next video, the skip of a refused queued video after about 2 s, "Couldn't play" in the mini player); audio that kept playing for 22 s with another app in front; Continue watching; local subscriptions and their feed; channel and playlist pages; History and Watch Later; a real download played from its file; blocking a keyword and a channel; links of every form; everything still there after a relaunch.

## Defects and fixes

| Id | Defect | Severity | Fix | Re-check on abbabff |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| qa-b3 | A 51-video playlist queue read "1 of 42+" | low | the queue states the playlist's size while pages load | pass, "1 of 51" |

| qa-c3 | iPad search results left a large empty area beside the first video card | low | channel and playlist rows span the width between runs of video cards; a lone video in such a run becomes a full-width row | the full-width rows passed on abbabff; the lone video was still a half-empty row and was fixed in 1adccfd |

## Not covered here: check on a real iPhone

The DEBUG hooks cannot type, swipe, scroll every list, press alert buttons or segmented controls, rotate, or lock the screen, so these were not checked:

- Search suggestions while typing, and applying a search filter.
- More pages of comments.
- The player's own buttons (plus and minus 10 s, the quality and speed menus) by hand; their actions were checked through script steps.
- Full screen and rotation, Picture in Picture, the lock screen and Control Center (Now Playing, Next), AirPlay, background audio with the screen locked.
- Deleting a download (a swipe), creating a local playlist (a text field).
- The Live and Playlists tabs of a channel (a segmented control).
- The lower part of Settings (Feed filters, Storage, About) and, at the largest text sizes, the Settings account section and the Downloads row in Library (code reading says both stack; not seen).
- iPad landscape.
